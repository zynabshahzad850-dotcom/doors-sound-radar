DOORS Sound Radar

Hear the entity before it hears you.
A free, open-source audio overlay for Roblox DOORS that listens to your speakers and instantly shows what's coming — and how to survive it.

⬇ Download for Windows - free Source / Python version

What it detects

RushDim the lights, get in a locker, close it.
AmbushAuto-detected from repeated Rush. Stay hidden through every pass.
ScreechTurn around and click it — in the light.
EyesLook away. Don't look. Don't attack.
FigureCrouch when it listens, hide under tables when it screeches.
SeekRun the path, jump, slide, dodge the lunge.
Electrical / FigmentBreak line of sight, dodge the sparks.
TimothStep around the flowers.
HaltWalk back to the previous room until the distortion fades.
GlitchHarmless bonus room — loot it.

How it works

  1. It records what your PC is playing (WASAPI loopback) — it never touches the game, reads no memory, injects nothing.
  2. DOORS plays the exact same sound files every time, so the app does template matching: you press REC once per entity (~3s of its sound) and it learns it forever.
  3. When a sample matches, a floating always-on-top window shows the entity, a confidence %, and the survival tip.
Why not a browser page? Web pages can't hear your system audio — it has to run on your PC. It's tiny, local, and offline.

Setup (1 minute — nothing to install)

  1. Download & unzip the Windows file.
  2. Double-click DOORS Sound Radar.exe. If Windows warns (new unsigned app): "More info" → "Run anyway".
  3. The overlay appears top-right and is already listening. Play DOORS in windowed/borderless mode.
  4. Teach it each entity once: the first time you HEAR e.g. Rush, hit its REC button. You can press up to ~2 seconds late — it also captures the 2.5s before you clicked. One click per sound, done forever.
  5. From then on it auto-alerts: entity name, confidence %, survival tip.
Why the one click? The entity audio belongs to the game's creators, so we can't bundle ripped sound files — but 3 seconds you capture yourself is all it needs, and that sample never leaves your PC.
